# Content grounding

The public website separates three kinds of statements.

## Current product statements

Ground these in the public Aegis repository, Marketplace listing, checked-in fixtures/tests, and observed 0.2.0 workflow. Examples include VS Code / GitHub Action / CLI surfaces, deterministic evidence, explicit model routes, controlled validation, secure fixes, policy outcomes, auditability, integrity records, and project security memory.

## Commercial statements

The current public commercial offer is derived from the 7 August 2026 institutional business plan:

- Community remains $0/open source.
- The immediate paid offer is a bounded 30-day design-partner cohort starting at $500.
- Team and Enterprise are conversations/pilots, not feature-complete subscription promises.
- Durable self-serve pricing is intentionally not published as a purchasable plan until the corresponding commercial capabilities ship.

## Company-direction statements

The public site may say Aegis is building toward trust infrastructure for software agents and may describe general trust primitives such as permission, evidence, verification, policy, recoverability, and memory.

The public site should not publish the detailed internal roadmap, proprietary corpus/data strategy, hidden benchmark design, or other implementation-level moat plans.
