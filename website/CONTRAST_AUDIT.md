# Contrast audit — final design tokens

Key WCAG contrast ratios for the institutional V5 palette:

- Near-black `#0A0C10` on ivory `#F7F2E8`: **17.54:1**
- Primary body `#2D2B26` on ivory `#F7F2E8`: **12.67:1**
- Secondary body `#4B4942` on ivory `#F7F2E8`: **8.07:1**
- Deep gold `#80570F` on ivory `#F7F2E8`: **5.72:1**
- Pale verification gold `#E9C16D` on near-black `#0A0C10`: **11.48:1**

The final system therefore does not use low-contrast decorative gold for body copy. Gold is reserved for short labels, verification states, rules, and structural emphasis.
